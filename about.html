About Jewellery by Riddhi

Every creation at Jewellery by Riddhi is handcrafted with care, attention to detail, and respect for traditional Indian & Maharashtrian craftsmanship. From Chokers, Necklaces, Mangalsutras, and Combo Sets to Naths, Bugadi, Mundavali, Bangles, and Rakhi—each piece is made to highlight your festive and bridal moments.

We also accept custom color requests and bulk orders (3 or more pieces) for weddings, family functions, and gifting.
