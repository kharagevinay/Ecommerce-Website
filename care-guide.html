Handmade Jewellery Care Guide

Simple care habits help preserve the shine, thread strength, and finish of your handmade jewellery.

1. Avoid Moisture, Perfume & Chemicals

Wear jewellery as the final step after applying perfume, hairspray, or creams. Keep away from water and sweat.

2. Airtight Storage

Store each piece individually inside an airtight zip-lock pouch or soft cloth box in a cool, dry place.

3. Soft Dry Wipe

Wipe gently with a soft, dry cotton or microfiber cloth after wearing; never use harsh liquids or brushes.

4. Gentle Handling

Handle adjustable doris, pearl strands, and delicate kundan/beadwork gently without tugging or crushing.
